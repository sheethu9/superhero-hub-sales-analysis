
# Superhero Hub – Sales Data Analysis

## Business Background
Superhero Hub is a retail company specializing in superhero-themed T-shirts and hoodies. The company offers products inspired by popular superheroes and caters to customers who enjoy superhero merchandise.

## Project Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze sales data and understand business performance.

## Key Business Questions
- What is the total revenue generated?
- Which products generate the highest sales?
- Which superhero-themed products are most popular?
- Which category performs better: T-shirts or hoodies?
- Which size has the highest demand?
- Which month generates the highest revenue?
- Which region contributes the most sales?
- What are the overall sales and profit trends?

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Power Query
- Charts and Dashboards
- Excel Formulas

## Expected Outcomes
- Identify best-selling products.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Track monthly sales performance.
- Identify high-performing product categories.
- Support data-driven business decisions.
